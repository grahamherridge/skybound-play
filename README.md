## Living-world graphics experiment

Play: https://grahamherridge.github.io/skybound-play/living-world/

An offline, cosmetic graphics experiment with an orchard village and Sky Post facade
in Big Sky, layered cloud banks, reactive plants and a moonlit harbour waterfront.
Compiled from private PR #38 at `4ec06e6`; no source files are published. The main
playable and Workshop keep their existing versions, including the homepage's newer
Stunt Yard/gantry Hook Turn work. This preview is for judging the graphics.

A/D or arrows steer; hold Space to thrust, release to coast; R retries. Touch controls are provided. Choose Lantern Reach / Collector to chase named postmarks and connected STACK x3 through x18. Breaking Flow keeps earned points and postmarks.

Stunt Run now uses only travelling gold pulses for its guiding flightline, without arrows or background dashes, with a matching overview route. Open Tricks & how to fly them for explanations of the four moves, controls and STYLE; opening the guide pauses safely.

Choose Stunt Show for unscored Explore or optional Stunt Run: genuine gap threads, near misses, late-burn saves and feather landings earn separate STYLE points and celebrations. A/D or arrows steer; hold Space to thrust; R retries. New Stunt Show calibration is 400/800/600/1500/2 only in that level; old defaults remain. Settle on the far-right final stage for an itemised receipt. The Great Crossing is rebuilt as a compact Stunt Yard: gold hook beside launch,180/110/86px climbing gaps, high dive, final bow, pulse practice line and an earned-action checklist, with unscored Explore and optional STYLE run. Hook Turn requires contouring a tagged physical gantry and a clean opposite exit; empty-sky reversals earn nothing; completed High Drop and Lantern Reach receipts add grade-aware dry humour. Hover remains deferred.

Choose Sandbox for Open Sky (free flight/dives), The Needle (precision with bypasses), or The Ribbon (3/6/18 real stamp trails). Candidate A is Sandbox-only; global Floaty defaults are preserved. In Sandbox hold A/D + Space while using 1-5 to select gravity/thrust/steering/braking/drag, O/P to adjust. The in-game Tuning Deck offers A/B, Mark/Return, Copy Settings and a clearly labelled demo-only Toybox. Demo fireworks never award points. Scene switches retain tuning; re-arm resets only the local attempt. Existing story modes, stacked rewards, SWIFT POST, CLEAN AIR, arch bonuses, mute and reduced motion remain available.

Compiled static playable only; development source/history remain private. No remote assets, tracking, runtime dependencies or backend.
