## New: Lantern Reach — the evening post

Choose Lantern Reach in the mode row. Explore a moonlit coastal harbour from the bell tower to the striped lighthouse. The 3,840 x 2,700 world holds eighteen stamps in six airborne phrases, scenic shelves, a tighter expert port and climbing harbour turns. There are thirteen aerial notices, including suspended and tiny-rocket signs. The heavy striped NO PARKING and PLEASE DON'T HIT THIS SIGN boards have static collision; decorative notices pass through. Clanks, sway and rocket bob use the existing bounded effects.

Visit the Bell arch, Tide arch and Lantern lens, then settle on the lighthouse balcony. Explorer needs no stamps. Collector needs all eighteen in order, with six independent Flow postmarks (5,400 points before a measured landing bonus, up to 5,700 total). Sprint needs any nine, with +1 second per missed stamp, adjusted time and a comparable session best. Early finishes explain missing requirements and let you return without losing valid progress. R retries the whole flight; no compulsory intermediate bank landings.

The High Drop retains its six-stamp Collector, any-three Sprint quota, +1s penalties, Flow rewards, receipts and twelve signs. Original Stamp sprint, Level 1/2, Big sky and both larger expeditions remain available. The flight lab and five live physics controls are unchanged.

Click Start flying. A/D or arrows steer; hold Space for thrust, release to coast. Touch controls work too. Flight instruments starts off; enable it in Flight controls for detailed numerical data. Objectives, timing, guidance and comic captions remain visible. Stamp sounds also mutes clanks. No installation, account or download required.

The courier starts at 1.25x visual scale, scarf 30/20, Floaty steering 250 and thrust buildup on. Compare size in Courier animation lab. Collision stays 28 x 36: enlarged helmets can visibly overlap contact faces and tight gaps. Physical-device readability, sound balance and enjoyment need human play-testing.

Only the compiled static playable is published here. No private source/history or infrastructure changes.
