Tricks & how to fly them

A/D or arrows steer. Hold Space to thrust, release to fall. R retries. Tricks recognise what you actually fly—there is no trick button.

Thread the Needle · precision
Line up with a marked opening, then fly all the way through and clear the far edge without clipping it. Start with the wide gap on the gold line; the higher gaps are harder.
Near Miss · audacity
Approach a solid obstacle at speed, skim close without touching, then fly clear. Try passing just above the marquee. Hovering beside a wall does not count; marked gaps already have their own threading reward.
Last-Second Burn · a proper save
Climb high, release Space and let a substantial fall build speed. Reignite late, leaving enough room for thrust to build and stop the fall before you hit anything. Try the high perch and open drop. A floor bounce is not a save.
Hook Turn · elegance
Build real sideways momentum, then hold the opposite steering direction to brake through a clean, airborne reversal. Accelerate away in the new direction without touching anything. Try it during an open-sky turn; tapping left/right while barely moving does not count. Fresh areas can earn it again.
Feather Landing · elegance
After a real flight, brake sideways and use short thrust burns to arrive gently on a floor or platform. Aim for very little downward or sideways speed on the first touch. Settling after a hard bounce does not count.

STYLE: link completed tricks within 10 seconds to build a multiplier, up to ×6. Each award is its base points × current STYLE. An impact or a long gap ends the active chain; earned points stay yours. Each obstacle rewards once per run, so seek fresh opportunities.

Explore acknowledges real moves without scoring. Stunt Run adds points; follow the gold line for a suggested route, then settle on the far-right stage for your receipt. The high dive and expert port are optional.
